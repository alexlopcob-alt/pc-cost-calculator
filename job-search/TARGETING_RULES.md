## Role scope (what we apply to)

Target titles, enterprise or mid-market, post-sale, individual contributor or player-coach:

- Senior / Strategic / Enterprise Customer Success Manager
- Key Account Manager / KAM / Gestor de Cuentas Clave / Grandes Cuentas (existing business, renewals and expansion)
- Senior / Strategic / Enterprise Account Manager
- Account Director / Client Director / Director de Cuentas, post-sale or retention led (new-business-only still excluded)
- Engagement Manager, Client Success Manager
- Customer Success Lead / Team Lead where the JD is 70%+ hands-on
- Head of Customer Success at a startup or scale-up (Series A–C)
- Renewals Manager

Fractional and interim lane (added 2026-10-01): fractional, interim or part-time Customer Success or Account Management engagements of any length, in Spain or remote EMEA. Scored with the same table. See "Fractional lane" below.

Preferred: SaaS, AI-native, fintech/payments, legaltech, data/intelligence, edtech/HR tech, compliance/GRC. Spanish + English bilingual asked for is a plus, not a requirement.

## Exclusions (auto-skip, no scoring)

- Any language requirement other than Spanish or English (German, Italian, French fluent, Portuguese fluent, Flemish, Romanian, etc.)
- New-business only, commission-only, SDR/BDR, field sales, channel-only
- Support, service desk, customer service team lead, implementation-only, onboarding specialist, CS Ops
- Junior / associate / "2–4 years" roles
- People-management-heavy Director/VP roles with 5+ direct reports
- Recruiter and staffing-agency postings with no named end client (score, but never send personal data)
- Published base below EUR 55,000 in Spain or GBP 65,000 for remote UK/US employers
- Permanent lane only: contract shorter than 12 months. The fractional lane has no minimum.
- Anything already applied to at the same company in the last 90 days

## Salary policy

- Madrid floor: EUR 60,000 base. Target ask: EUR 65,000–75,000 base, OTE above.
- Remote EMEA with UK/US employer: GBP 75,000 / EUR 85,000 base ask; floor GBP 65,000.
- Optional salary fields stay blank. Mandatory fields get the target ask, never the floor.
- Alex negotiates every offer personally.

## Volume and cadence (quality over quantity)

- Max 6 submissions per day, all at fit 7+, Madrid first.
- Every submission at fit 8+ gets a hiring-manager or recruiter touch within 24 hours, or it is not submitted.
- Every submission gets a follow-up at day 7 and day 14 if no human reply.
- LinkedIn connection requests: max 10 per day, sent by Alex or in Alex's own Chrome session, never headless. Keep pending invites under 50.
- Direct email to hiring managers: max 10 per day.
- Fractional lane: up to 5 pitches per week, on Spain or remote-EMEA roles open 30+ days at fit 7+, plus any listing that is explicitly fractional, interim or part-time.

## Touch channel (the part that produces interviews)

Six of the eight interviews since 24 Aug came from a warm route or a hiring-manager note. ATS-only submissions convert at about one in thirty. So the touch is not optional at fit 7+, and the order is:

1. Email to the hiring manager or CS lead, address resolved through Apollo (paid, active again 1 Oct), the company email pattern, or a verified guess. Email does not depend on the Chrome extension and does not count against LinkedIn limits.
2. LinkedIn connection note (300 characters, personalised) in Alex's own Chrome session when no address resolves. LinkedIn Premium Career covers the personalised-note allowance; Sales Navigator is not used.
3. Warm intro through a former colleague when one exists at the company. This beats both of the above.

Every note is a Gmail draft or a queued LinkedIn note for Alex to send. Automation never sends to a third party.
