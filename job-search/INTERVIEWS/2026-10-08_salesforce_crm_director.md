## What a Customer Risk Manager does (frame every answer around this)
The role takes over at-risk strategic accounts across products. It finds the root cause of the risk, builds a recovery plan with the account team, success, product and executives, and turns attrition risk into retained ARR. It is less about relationship management and more about structured escalation, deal desk and exec alignment.
