## Scenario 1: red account escalation

Think out loud. They score *reasoning*, not the answer (Michal's words).

**Open with:** "Let me restate what I heard, ask two questions, then commit to a plan and name the trade-off."

1. **Contain (today).** One owner (me), one channel, a holding message to the customer exec today. No fix date I don't control.
2. **Diagnose.** Symptom vs root cause. State assumptions aloud. What changed, including on their side (release, reorg, new exec, go-live date).
3. **Mobilise.** Support engineering, TA/architect, AE, exec sponsor, each a named owner. I own the narrative.
4. **Three tracks, three clocks.** Technical, relationship, commercial. Different owners, never merged into one update.
5. **Commit to cadence, not dates.** "Relief ≠ resolution": a workaround is not closure.
6. **Exec update format (EN/ES):** Situation (one sentence, no adjectives) → Business impact (who can't do what) → Ruled out → Actions in flight with owners → Next checkpoint (date and time, always).
7. **Close the loop.** Why did this reach us through the customer's exec rather than our own signals? That is the systemic fix, and the role owns it.

**Escalation trigger:** pre-agree it with the AE and CSM ("if X by Thursday, VP Customer Success is looped"). Don't escalate by reflex.
