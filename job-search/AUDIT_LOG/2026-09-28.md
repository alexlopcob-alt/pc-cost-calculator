## Root causes of the rejection rate

1. Volume-first ATS submissions with no human touch. The outreach task was missing or frozen on most days from 21 Sep onward.
2. London bias in the pipeline because London boards were technically easy to reach; Madrid roles sit behind LinkedIn Apply, InfoJobs and Workday walls the headless applier cannot pass.
3. Scattered scope (CSM, AM, Account Director, Engagement Manager, Renewals Ops, Implementation, Risk Manager, Channel). Screeners saw no clear profile.
4. Fact drift across 50 CVs (ARR, account count, CrossKnowledge start year, missing Eukleia role, English "native" vs "fluent"). Sixteen wrong CVs are with employers, including Salesforce.
5. Machine reliability: laptop sleep, stuck task chats, expired auth, permission hangs. The Engine spent Alex's attention instead of saving it.
6. Salary band mismatch on Spanish mid-market employers (CTAIMA, GuruSup at 52k vs 60k floor). International hubs in Madrid pay the target band; local mid-market does not.
