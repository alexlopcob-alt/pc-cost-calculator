00 / GROUND TRUTH

Four findings that should change the plan

I'd rather front-load these than bury them under a contact list. Each one is load-bearing for how you spend the next 11 days.

1. The date is right, and that's genuinely lucky. The Spanish GP runs 11–13 September 2026 at MADRING (IFEMA). Friday 11 September is practice day — first track action is late morning/afternoon. A 09:00–10:30 slot sits in real dead time for ticket-holders, and doesn't compete with the race itself. Verified: madring.com.
2. Your economics can't fund a hotel sales cycle. 30 seats at €89–129 is roughly €2,700–3,900 gross. Hotel MICE teams quote on 3–6 month lead times and rank inbound by F&B spend. A €3k event with 11 days' notice will not clear their internal priority filter, and chasing it burns the days you need for selling. Treat venue partnership as a parallel track with a hard stop, not the critical path.
3. Your buyer is a Madrid resident, not an F1 tourist. Official F1 Experiences hospitality for the 2026 Spanish GP is already sold out — so high-spend visitors exist, but they arrive on fixed, pre-paid itineraries and are jet-lagged on Friday morning. The realistic 09:00 Friday buyer is a Madrid-based high earner who wants the race-week charge without a grandstand seat. Sell to Salamanca and Chamberí first; sell to visitors second.
4. Your actual leverage with venues is dead inventory. Madrid hotel rooftops don't open to the public until roughly midday. At 09:00 on a Friday, a rooftop generates precisely zero revenue. That is a far stronger opening than "partnership" as an abstraction — you are asking to activate a space during hours it currently earns nothing, on the single highest-visibility weekend of the city's year. Lead every venue email with that.
Operational filter — apply before you fall in love with a rooftop. 30 people lying on mats needs roughly 60–75 m² of clear, flat, level floor (0.6 × 1.8 m per mat plus circulation and a facilitator lane). Pool decks, fixed Balinese beds, planters and split levels kill capacity fast. Ask every venue for clear floor area, not total area.

2 clubs × internal comms ≈ 6–10 seats

Your own direct network, worked manually

Unglamorous and routinely skipped in favour of partnership decks. 2 communities × ~1,000 reach × 0.3% ≈ 6 seats
